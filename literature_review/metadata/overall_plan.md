# Literature Review Overall Plan

Project: Automated Parking Lot Occupancy and Tracking System

## Review scope

This literature review focuses on methods that can support a surveillance-based parking occupancy system:

- real-time vehicle/object detection
- multi-object tracking in video
- robustness to low light, rain, fog, and sensor degradation
- generalization across camera views and deployment environments
- open-world or open-vocabulary perception for adaptable class definitions

## Inclusion criteria

Primary criteria:

- Published in 2024-2025.
- Published in CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML, SIGGRAPH, or similarly high-impact computer vision venues.
- Technically relevant to parking lot occupancy detection and tracking, even if the paper is not specifically about parking lots.

Secondary criteria:

- Preference for papers with code, datasets, benchmarks, or deployment-oriented insights.
- Preference for methods that can be integrated into an overhead-camera pipeline.
- Exclude papers whose contribution is unrelated to visual detection, tracking, weather robustness, or scene understanding.

## Expected output

- A candidate paper pool with downloaded PDFs.
- Metadata and BibTeX entries.
- Notes for selected core papers.
- A final literature review draft with a comparison table.
